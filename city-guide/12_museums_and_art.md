Museums & Art

San Francisco has a museum culture of genuine depth, anchored by several world-class institutions.

### San Francisco Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA)

Reopened in 2016 after a major expansion, SFMOMA is one of the finest modern and contemporary art museums in the United States. The permanent collection is particularly strong in photography, abstract expressionism, and Bay Area artists. The building itself, designed by Snøhetta, is architecturally significant.

### de Young Museum

In Golden Gate Park, the de Young holds the Fine Arts Museums of San Francisco's collection of American art, textiles, and international art from Africa, Oceania, and the Americas. The building, designed by Herzog & de Meuron, is striking in its weathered copper cladding. The observation tower is free and offers panoramic views of the park.

### California Academy of Sciences

Also in Golden Gate Park, the Academy houses a natural history museum, a planetarium, and a four-story living rainforest under a living roof designed by Renzo Piano. The rooftop garden alone is worth the visit. Evening NightLife events (18+) run on Thursday evenings.

### The Legion of Honor

In Lincoln Park near Land's End, the Legion of Honor houses European paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts in a beautiful Beaux-Arts building modeled on the Palais de la Légion d'honneur in Paris. The setting, overlooking the Pacific, is extraordinary.

### The Exploratorium

At Pier 15 on the Embarcadero, the Exploratorium is a science museum focused on hands-on learning that has been influencing science education worldwide since 1969. It is equally engaging for adults and children.

### The Museum of the African Diaspora (MoAD)

Near SFMOMA in SoMa, MoAD focuses on the history and art of the African diaspora with a strong contemporary programming component.

### Asian Art Museum

Near City Hall, the Asian Art Museum holds one of the most comprehensive Asian art collections outside Asia, spanning 6,000 years and 40 countries.

### The Walt Disney Family Museum

In the Presidio, this privately funded museum tells the story of Walt Disney's life and work through extensive archival material, models, and interactive exhibits. Even visitors with no particular attachment to Disney tend to find it remarkable.

### Gallery Scene

The city has a substantial commercial gallery scene, concentrated in SoMa around SFMOMA and in the Minnesota Street Project in Dogpatch, which houses multiple galleries in a purpose-built complex.
