# Getting Around: GoTriangle & Regional Transit

GoTriangle is the regional transit authority serving Wake, Durham, and Orange counties —
the three-county area that encompasses Raleigh, Durham, Chapel Hill, and Research Triangle
Park. While GoRaleigh handles local city bus service within Raleigh itself, GoTriangle
operates the longer regional express routes that connect Raleigh to RTP, Durham, and the
other Triangle cities.

For workshop participants whose final destination is the **NC Biotech Center in Research
Triangle Park**, GoTriangle is the most relevant transit option.

**Route 100 — Raleigh to Research Triangle Park:**
The most important GoTriangle route for this workshop is Route 100, the RTP Express.
Departures are from the GoRaleigh/GoTriangle Moore Square Transit Center (downtown
Raleigh) and run non-stop to the Research Triangle Park area, including a stop near the
NC Biotech Center campus. Journey time: approximately 35 minutes. Fare: $2.25. The bus
departs at the top and bottom of the hour during weekday service hours (roughly
6:30am–7:30pm). Check the GoTriangle app or website for the exact schedule on event day.

**Route 400 — Durham–Raleigh Express:**
Route 400 connects downtown Durham to downtown Raleigh via US-15-501 and I-40, stopping
at both the Durham Transit Center and the GoRaleigh Moore Square Transit Center. Journey
time: approximately 50 minutes. Useful if attendees are staying in Durham.

**The GoTriangle Go! app:**
GoTriangle's mobile app (available on iOS and Android) is the recommended way to purchase
fares, track real-time bus locations, and plan multi-leg regional trips. It covers both
GoRaleigh and GoTriangle services in a single interface. You can load a digital pass before
you leave the workshop venue and use it immediately.

**Raleigh Union Station:**
Raleigh Union Station, at 510 West Martin Street in the Warehouse District, is the city's
Amtrak station. The Piedmont and Carolinian Amtrak trains stop here, connecting Raleigh to
Charlotte (three hours) and Washington DC (seven hours). Regional bus services also depart
from the station. For workshop participants arriving by train from Charlotte or Durham,
Union Station is your entry point into the city, conveniently located at the northern edge
of the Warehouse District.

**Future transit:**
The Wake County Transit Plan, approved by voters in 2016, funds a significant expansion of
GoTriangle and GoRaleigh services, including new Bus Rapid Transit lines along the major
north-south and east-west corridors. The first BRT line on New Bern Avenue and Western
Boulevard is under development. A commuter rail line between Durham and Raleigh (the
Durham-Orange Light Rail's successor project) remains in long-term planning.

**Practical notes for quest participants:**
If you are taking GoTriangle Route 100 to RTP at the end of a quest, give yourself ten
minutes of buffer from the Moore Square Transit Center. The stop for Route 100 is on the
east side of the transit center on Blount Street; signs at the shelter indicate which bay
serves regional routes versus city routes.
