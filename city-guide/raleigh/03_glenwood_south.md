# Glenwood South

Glenwood South is the name given to the stretch of Glenwood Avenue north of Peace Street,
running from roughly Morgan Street up to Fairview Road. It is Raleigh's premier
entertainment and dining corridor: tree-lined, walkable, lively on evenings and weekends,
and home to a dense collection of restaurants, bars, rooftop terraces, and boutique shops
that draw both locals and visitors.

The neighbourhood occupies what was once the edge of downtown, separated from the urban
core by the rail corridor and the old Peace Street boundary. Its transformation began in
the 1990s and accelerated through the 2000s and 2010s as former storefronts were
repurposed into bars and restaurants and new construction brought mixed-use buildings
with ground-floor retail. Today Glenwood South feels settled and confident rather than
gentrifying — a destination in its own right.

**Getting there** is easy from Moore Square. The GoRaleigh Route 11 bus runs from the
Moore Square Transit Center along Glenwood Avenue every thirty minutes during the day
(18-minute ride, $1.25). Capital Bikeshare has a dock at Moore Square and stations at
505 Glenwood Avenue; the ride takes about twelve minutes and is mostly flat. Rideshare
from Moore Square runs eight to ten minutes. Walking the 1.1 miles takes about fifteen
minutes and is pleasant in good weather — you pass through the transition zone where
downtown gives way to the entertainment strip.

**Food and drink** are the main draws. Standards worth knowing: Beasley's Chicken + Honey
(fried chicken and craft beer), Watts & Ward (cocktails in a converted bank vault), Hummingbird (eclectic small plates), and Crawford and Son (farm-to-table just at the edge
of the district). On warm evenings the rooftop at Foundation Bar fills early; the basement
bar at 311 West Martin draws a later crowd.

**Shopping** on Glenwood South leans toward independent boutiques: Tradecraft (vintage
furniture and objects), Oak & Dagger (menswear), and a rotating cast of pop-up shops in
the smaller storefronts. The area is not a mall; it is a neighbourhood that happens to have
excellent retail embedded in it.

**Events**: Glenwood South hosts First Friday gallery and bar crawls at the start of each
month, outdoor yoga on the median in the summer, and the Glenwood Grilling Competition in
late August. The district is most alive from Thursday through Saturday evenings.

The **Glenwood South Business Association** was established in 1987 to coordinate
improvement efforts across the then-struggling strip. Early members pooled funds for
streetlighting and landscaping, a model that became the template for several other Raleigh
business improvement districts. Archives of the association's founding meetings, held at
the old Glenwood Hotel on the corner of Peace and Glenwood, describe the optimism of that
era in detail.

For workshop attendees passing through: the Capital Bikeshare dock at 505 Glenwood Avenue
is on the east side of the street just past Peace. The GoRaleigh Route 11 stop is at
Glenwood and Clark, northbound and southbound. Rideshare pickup is easiest from the
Cabernet Grill parking area on the west side.
