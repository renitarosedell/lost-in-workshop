# Annual Events & Festivals

Raleigh has developed a strong calendar of annual events, many of them rooted in the city's
food, music, and technology culture. Knowing which events are running during your visit
can add unexpected colour to a trip that might otherwise be purely professional.

**Hopscotch Music Festival** (September): Three days of music spread across indoor and
outdoor venues across downtown Raleigh, with an emphasis on indie rock, hip-hop, and
experimental music. It has grown from a regional curiosity into one of the better mid-size
music festivals in the country. The outdoor stage in Raleigh's City Plaza is free; indoor
venues are ticketed.

**Wide Open Bluegrass** (late September / October): The festival arm of the International
Bluegrass Music Association's annual conference, which Raleigh hosts. It brings the best
traditional and contemporary bluegrass acts to outdoor stages downtown for a free Friday
evening concert and a paid Saturday all-day festival. Worth attending if you are in town.

**Brewgaloo** (April): A craft beer festival in City Plaza, featuring over 100 North
Carolina breweries. One of the largest beer festivals in the state and consistently
well-organized.

**North Carolina State Fair** (October): The annual state fair at the NC State Fairgrounds
on Western Boulevard is one of the largest state fairs in the country, attracting over a
million visitors over ten days. Agriculture, rides, livestock competitions, and the
signature NC fair foods (deep-fried everything; the fried Oreos are not a myth).

**First Friday** (monthly): Downtown Raleigh's gallery and bar crawl, the first Friday
of every month. Galleries open their doors, bars offer specials, and the sidewalks of
Glenwood South and the Warehouse District fill with people. No ticket required; just show
up.

**Artsplosure** (May): A two-day outdoor arts festival in and around Moore Square and City
Plaza, featuring visual art, craft, live music, and street food. Free to attend; one of the
city's most family-friendly annual events.

**Dreamville Festival** (Spring): The music festival associated with Dreamville Records
(founded by J. Cole and Ibrahim Hamad, both with North Carolina roots) has been held at
Dorothea Dix Park since 2019 and draws major hip-hop and R&B artists. Ticketed; sells out.

**Raleigh Oyster Festival** (November): An annual gathering of oyster purveyors, shuckers,
and enthusiasts at a location that varies year to year in the warehouse district area. A
good reason to be in Raleigh in early November.

**International Festival** (October): A multi-day celebration of Raleigh's growing
immigrant communities at the Raleigh Convention Center, featuring food, dance, and cultural
displays from dozens of countries. The Triangle's diversity — built partly on the
international workforce drawn to RTP — is well represented here.

For workshop participants: the Artsplosure spring festival and First Friday events require
no planning; they are simply ambient to being downtown in Raleigh at the right time.
