# Practical Information

## Weather

Raleigh has a humid subtropical climate with four distinct seasons. Brief summary:

- **Spring (March–May)**: The best time to visit. Temperatures 55–75°F (13–24°C), blooming
  dogwood and azalea, low humidity, occasional afternoon thunderstorms.
- **Summer (June–August)**: Hot and humid. Temperatures 85–95°F (29–35°C). High humidity
  makes it feel warmer. Afternoon thunderstorms are common and brief. Shade and
  air conditioning are your friends.
- **Fall (September–November)**: Excellent. Temperatures drop to 55–75°F (13–24°C) by
  October; leaf colour peaks mid-October. Light jacket weather by November.
- **Winter (December–February)**: Mild by northern standards (40–55°F / 4–13°C during the
  day) but cold enough to feel uncomfortable without a proper coat. Snow falls 1–4 times
  per year, typically 1–3 inches, and causes city-wide disruption disproportionate to the
  amount that actually falls.

## Safety

Raleigh is a generally safe city for a major American metro area. The downtown core,
Glenwood South, and the Warehouse District are active and well-lit in the evenings and are
safe to walk at night. Standard city precautions apply: keep your phone and bag aware of
your surroundings, use rideshare or walk in groups late at night, avoid the darker stretches
of the South Saunders corridor after dark.

The areas around the State Capitol and Bicentennial Plaza are heavily frequented during the
day and quiet but safe in the evening.

## Tipping

North Carolina follows standard American tipping conventions:
- Sit-down restaurant: 18–20% of the pre-tax bill is the local norm; 22% is generous.
- Counter service (coffee shop, bakery): rounding up or a dollar per item is typical.
- Rideshare: 15–20% is encouraged; the apps prompt you after the ride.
- Hotel housekeeping: $2–5 per night, left in the room.
- Delivery (DoorDash, Uber Eats): 15–20% of the order total.

## Connectivity

Raleigh has excellent mobile coverage on all major US carriers. Downtown has several free
municipal WiFi zones (City Plaza, Bicentennial Plaza). Most coffee shops offer free WiFi.
The convention centre and hotel conference facilities have dedicated event networks; the
NC Biotech Center provides event WiFi details at registration.

## Currency and payments

The United States uses dollars. Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are universally
accepted in Raleigh. Contactless payment (Apple Pay, Google Pay) is accepted at most
businesses. ATMs are plentiful downtown; bank ATMs charge no fee for account holders and
$2–4 for non-customers. Carry a small amount of cash ($20–40) for bus fares, tip-only
situations, and the occasional cash-only food vendor at markets.
