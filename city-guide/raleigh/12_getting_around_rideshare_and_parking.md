# Getting Around: Rideshare & Parking

Rideshare — primarily Uber and Lyft, with Waymo expanding into the Triangle area —
is the most flexible and often the fastest point-to-point transport option in Raleigh,
particularly for legs that don't align with bus routes or for journeys that require
carrying bags or navigating parts of the city with limited bike infrastructure.

**Typical wait times by area (daytime, non-peak):**
- Downtown core (Moore Square, Nash Square, City Market): 3–5 minutes.
- Glenwood South: 4–8 minutes.
- Museum District / Bicentennial Plaza: 4–7 minutes.
- Warehouse District / CAM Raleigh: 5–9 minutes.
- Boylan Heights: 6–12 minutes (residential area, fewer drivers circulating).
- Research Triangle Park: 10–20 minutes (suburban campus, few drivers; plan ahead).

During peak hours (7:30–9:00am and 4:30–6:30pm weekdays), wait times in the downtown
core can extend to 10–15 minutes and surge pricing may apply. Workshop participants
should allow extra buffer for rideshare legs during these windows.

**Pickup logistics in downtown Raleigh:**
The downtown grid has several streets with no-standing restrictions during peak hours.
The most reliable pickup locations are:
- Moore Square: the east side of Blount Street between Hargett and Martin.
- Fayetteville Street: pull-over spots near the Marriott City Center.
- Warehouse District: South Blount Street in front of Transfer Co. Food Hall.
- Glenwood South: the parking area near the Cabernet Grill on the west side.

If a driver cancels, keep your pickup pin in the same location and retry — typically a
fresh match arrives within a minute or two.

**Parking** in downtown Raleigh is managed by a mix of city-run decks and private surface
lots. If you are arriving by car:
- The City of Raleigh operates several affordable decks, including the Davie Street
  Parking Deck (near the Warehouse District, $1/hour) and the Fayetteville Street Deck
  (near Moore Square, $2/hour after the first hour free).
- Private surface lots on the periphery of downtown typically charge $5–10 per event.
- Street parking (metered, $1.50/hour) is available on most downtown streets but is
  hard to find near Moore Square on weekday mornings.

**Parking for Research Triangle Park:**
The NC Biotech Center and most RTP campuses have generous free surface parking. If driving
to the workshop, arrive early and use the main visitor lot at the Biotech Center entrance.

**Ride-sharing to RTP:**
If using rideshare for the final quest leg to the NC Biotech Center, note that Research
Triangle Park is a campus environment where rideshare drivers may not know the specific
building locations. Providing the full address ("15 T.W. Alexander Drive, Research
Triangle Park") rather than just "NC Biotech Center" gets you a more accurate drop-off.
Expect fares of $18–28 from downtown Raleigh to RTP, depending on surge pricing.
