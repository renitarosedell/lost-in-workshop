Coffee Culture

San Francisco is one of the birthplaces of the third-wave coffee movement, and the city remains serious about its coffee.

**Ritual Coffee Roasters** was among the first roasters to establish direct relationships with coffee farmers and roast with precision. Multiple locations across the city; the Valencia Street original is the flagship.

**Blue Bottle Coffee** started in Oakland but is closely associated with San Francisco's coffee scene. The Hayes Valley kiosk was its first permanent location. Now a global brand, but the quality remains high.

**Sightglass Coffee** in SoMa occupies a beautiful double-height industrial space and roasts on-site. One of the best environments in the city for working or meeting.

**Verve Coffee Roasters** transplanted from Santa Cruz and brought excellent single-origin espresso with it.

**Linea Caffe** near the Mission is a small, serious espresso bar with no Wi-Fi and no distractions — just very good coffee.

For a more traditional experience, the Italian cafes of North Beach — Caffe Trieste and Caffe Greco — have been pulling espresso since before specialty coffee was a concept. Caffe Trieste claims to have been the first espresso house on the West Coast.
