# Get an Azure Subscription

In case you do not have an Azure subscription, this page shows you how to get one for the workshop.

---

## Azure Classroom <Badge type="tip" text="Recommended for this event" />

::: info This offer applies to Lost in Workshop events run by the Global AI Community.
You will receive **$10 USD credit valid for 5 days**, which is plenty for this workshop.
:::

### Step 1 - Request subscription

- Navigate to **[https://aka.ms/JoinEduLab](https://aka.ms/JoinEduLab)**
- Sign in with a **Microsoft account** or **GitHub account**

![Azure Classroom signup form](https://kind-sea-0cbd10e03.7.azurestaticapps.net/assets/001.kySF7vRn.png)

- Enter the **invite code** provided by your organiser (printed on your card, or posted in the event Slack/Teams)
- Click **Redeem**
- Wait for the activation email to arrive in your inbox

::: tip Where is my invite code?
Your facilitator will hand out invite codes at the start of the event. If you haven't received one, ask at the desk.
:::

---

### Step 2 - Activate subscription

- Check your email for a message with the subject **"You have a request to take ownership of an Azure subscription"**

![Activation email screenshot](https://kind-sea-0cbd10e03.7.azurestaticapps.net/assets/002.Cyr16cBL.png)

- Click **Accept Ownership**
- Sign in with the **same account** you used in step 1
- After login, click **Accept** to join the workshop tenant
- Choose a subscription name (or keep the suggestion) and click **Review + Accept**, then **Accept**
- Your subscription is now being created

---

### Step 3 - Happy hacking!

- Subscription creation takes **2–5 minutes**
- You will receive a second email with the subject **"Your Azure subscription is ready"**
- You can also watch the progress under the **Notifications** bell in the Azure portal
- Click **"Go to subscription"** in the notification or **"Go to the Azure portal"** in the email

::: tip Expected result
You can see your subscription at [portal.azure.com](https://portal.azure.com). The subscription is listed under **Subscriptions** in the top search bar.
:::

---

### Tips & Tricks

#### Can't find your subscription?

- Your subscription is created in the **"Global AI Community"** tenant/directory
- You may need to **switch directories**: click your account avatar in the top-right → **Switch directory**
- Or go directly to [portal.azure.com/#settings/directory](https://portal.azure.com/#settings/directory) to switch
- Check your **Subscription filter**: [portal.azure.com/#view/Microsoft_Azure_Billing/SubscriptionsBladeV2](https://portal.azure.com/#view/Microsoft_Azure_Billing/SubscriptionsBladeV2)

#### Invite code already redeemed?

Ask your facilitator for a fresh code, as each code is single-use.

## Azure Free Trial <Badge type="info" text="If you have no invite code" />

If you are following this workshop independently (not at a live event), you can sign up for the Azure free trial:

- Go to **[azure.microsoft.com/free](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/purchase-options/azure-account)**
- Sign in with a Microsoft account (or create one - it takes two minutes)
- You receive **$200 USD credit for 30 days**

::: warning Credit card required for free trial
The Azure free trial requires a credit card for identity verification. You will **not** be charged during the trial period.
:::
